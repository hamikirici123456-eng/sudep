# KORVA website notes

KORVA is a proposed working brand name for this website concept. Company registration, Turkish trademark clearance, and domain availability have not been confirmed.

## Before publishing

- Set the real sales email in `CONTACT.email` in `assets/site.js`. The contact form uses the visitor's email client and does not store submissions.
- Add the company's verified phone, address, legal name, and any privacy notices that apply to the final form setup.
- Replace the current third-party product and industrial photos with images the company owns or has permission to use. Their source URLs are in `assets/site.css`.
- Add only verified company details, product certificates, technical specifications, completed projects, and customer references.
- Review product claims, technical documents, and the final domain with the responsible engineering and legal teams.
